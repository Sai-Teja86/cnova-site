Compliance

Built for Indian regulations

CERT-In, the DPDP Act, RBI, SEBI and IRDAI all expect you to control privileged access and prove it. Vaultgate gives you the controls they ask for and the evidence your auditors check, on an appliance in your own data centre.

CERT-InDPDP ActRBISEBIIRDAI ISO 27001Data residencyYour part

Regulations apply to your organisation, not to a product, and no regulator certifies PAM tools. What follows maps each framework's privileged access expectations to what Vaultgate does. It is a guide for your compliance team, not legal advice: check the current text of each regulation, which regulators revise.

CERT-In Directions (April 2022)

Issued under section 70B of the IT Act and binding on service providers, intermediaries, data centres and body corporates in India.

RequirementEnable logs on all ICT systems and keep them securely for a rolling 180 days, within Indian jurisdiction
How Vaultgate helpsThe audit log is never deleted. Session recordings can't be set to less than 180 days. Everything stays on the appliance in your own data centre
RequirementSynchronise clocks to NIC or NPL time servers, or to servers traceable to them
How Vaultgate helpsTime page: set NTP servers from the console (one click for NIC and NPL), see the offset and the servers in use, and get an alert if the clock loses sync
RequirementReport incidents within 6 hours
How Vaultgate helpsAlerts by email as things happen, every audit event forwarded to your SOC at once, and the full trail of who did what, ready for the report
RequirementProvide logs to CERT-In on request
How Vaultgate helpsExport the audit log as CSV, or produce a PDF report for any period. Recordings can be exported as evidence packages

Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Rules 2025

Applies to every organisation processing digital personal data in India. Most duties under the Rules take effect in phases after November 2025.

RequirementReasonable security safeguards, including access control
How Vaultgate helpsNobody needs to know server passwords: Vaultgate holds them encrypted, injects them into sessions and changes them after use. Access is by policy, approval and time window
RequirementVisibility of access to personal data, to detect and investigate unauthorised access
How Vaultgate helpsEvery privileged session is recorded, with typed commands for SSH and keystrokes for remote desktops. Sign-ins, approvals and password reveals are all audited
RequirementKeep the logs for at least one year
How Vaultgate helpsThe audit log is kept for the life of the appliance. Set recordings to 365 days or more, or keep them forever
RequirementNotify the Board and affected people of a breach
How Vaultgate helpsThe recorded sessions and audit trail show exactly which systems were reached, by whom and when, which scopes the notification

Reserve Bank of India

Banks, NBFCs, payment system operators and co-operative banks: the Master Direction on IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices (2023), the Cyber Security Framework for banks, and the IT outsourcing direction.

RequirementPrivileged access management: vaulted and rotated administrator passwords
How Vaultgate helpsVault with AES-256-GCM envelope encryption; automatic rotation for servers, databases and network devices; passwords changed after each use
RequirementMulti-factor authentication for privileged users
How Vaultgate helpsAuthenticator codes that administrators can require, and single sign-on with your own identity provider
RequirementMonitoring and recording of privileged activity
How Vaultgate helpsSSH, RDP and VNC sessions recorded and searchable; live monitoring; sessions ended on blocked commands or keywords
RequirementLeast privilege and need-based, time-bound access
How Vaultgate helpsApproval workflows with time windows; personal just-in-time accounts created for a session and removed after it
RequirementPeriodic review of privileged access
How Vaultgate helpsAn auditor role that can see and report but not change anything; period reports of users, access, usage and findings
RequirementLogs to the bank's SOC
How Vaultgate helpsEvery audit event forwarded to your SIEM over syslog, encrypted and in order
RequirementRight to audit the vendor and continuity of critical services
How Vaultgate helpsA self-hosted appliance under your control, with standby and automatic failover, encrypted backups and an offline recovery kit

SEBI Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF, 2024)

Stock brokers, depositories, exchanges, asset management companies and other SEBI-regulated entities.

RequirementPrivileged identity and access management, with MFA
How Vaultgate helpsVault, rotation, approvals, MFA and recorded sessions for every administrator, in one appliance
RequirementLogs collected and monitored by a SOC
How Vaultgate helpsReal-time forwarding of every audit event to your SIEM, with the tamper-evident chain hash in each message
RequirementLogs and data kept in India
How Vaultgate helpsSelf-hosted: no cloud service, no data leaves your network
RequirementPeriodic access reviews
How Vaultgate helpsAuditor role and period reports

IRDAI Information and Cyber Security Guidelines (2023)

Insurers and insurance intermediaries.

RequirementControl and monitoring of privileged and administrative access
How Vaultgate helpsBrokered, recorded sessions with no password handed to people; approvals and MFA
RequirementLogs kept for 180 days, in India, with time synchronised
How Vaultgate helpsAudit log never deleted, recordings at least 180 days, NTP status and alerts, all on your own appliance

Your data stays in your network

Wherever you run Vaultgate, in India or anywhere else, everything it holds stays on your own appliance. That is what makes data residency rules straightforward to meet.

DeploymentAn appliance on your own hardware or hypervisor, in your own data centre. No cloud service
What leaves the applianceNothing you don't send: email alerts to your mail server, events to your SIEM, backups to where you put them. The daily license check-in sends only the appliance's identity, version and usage counts, and offline activation removes it
EvidenceRecordings and keystroke logs are encrypted on the appliance, the audit log is tamper-evident, and exports are limited to people you allow and audited

What stays your part

Policies and reviewsDeciding who should have access, and reviewing it on schedule. Vaultgate enforces and records it
Incident reportingReporting to CERT-In, the Data Protection Board or your regulator. Vaultgate gives you the facts quickly
Retention settingsChoosing recording retention beyond the 180-day minimum to match your regulator and your own policy
Everything outside privileged accessEndpoint, network and application controls, and the rest of your security programme
