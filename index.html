The problem Vaultgate solves

Administrator and service passwords are the keys to every system you run. In most companies they live in spreadsheets, chat messages and people's heads. They rarely change, they are shared between people, and nobody can say who used them or what was done.

Vaultgate puts those passwords in one encrypted vault, changes them automatically, and lets people use them without seeing them. Every session goes through the gateway, is checked against policy, recorded, and written to an audit trail that shows any tampering.
